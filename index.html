Column legend — what each metric means and why it matters

ColumnWhat it isWhy it matters
LiveLatest traded priceThe baseline every other column is measured against.
TrendA dot plot: gray dot = 200 SMA, gold dot = 50 SMA, bright dot = live price, colored by statusA one-glance read on where price sits relative to both trend lines, without reading three separate numbers.
200 SMAAverage close over the trailing ~300 calendar days (≈200 trading days)The long-term trend line. Price below it usually means the stock is "cheap" relative to its own recent history; well above it means it's extended.
50 SMAAverage close over the trailing ~75 calendar days (≈50 trading days)The medium-term trend. Comparing it to the 200 SMA shows whether momentum is building (50 above 200, a "golden cross" setup) or fading (50 below 200, a "death cross" setup).
RSI14Relative Strength Index over 14 days, 0–100 scaleMomentum gauge. Below ~30–40 = oversold (selling may be overdone); above ~70 = overbought (buying may be overdone). Used here as the trigger that turns a dip into a "STRIKE."
Vol Strength(This ticker's volume ÷ its average volume) ÷ (SPY's volume ÷ its average volume)Shows if the stock is trading unusually heavy or light relative to the whole market today. Above 1x on a dip = real, market-wide-adjusted selling pressure, not just noise.
Struct. Risk(50 SMA − 200 SMA) ÷ 200 SMANegative means the medium-term trend is sitting below the long-term trend — a structural warning that the recent move down may not just be a dip. Positive means medium-term momentum is running ahead of the long-term trend.
Dist→Floor(Live − 200 SMA) ÷ 200 SMAHow far price sits above or below its long-term "floor." Deeply negative = far below trend (higher risk, but potentially higher reward if it's a real dip); strongly positive = extended above trend.
Target / %→TargetYour own manually-set entry price, and how far live price is from itYour personal buy plan, not a model output — the dashboard doesn't set this for you, it just tracks the gap.
StopYour own manually-set exit priceYour risk limit if the thesis breaks — also not calculated by the tool, just tracked here for visibility.
EarningsNext scheduled earnings date (via the "Info" button, Finnhub)Flagged (⚠) when within 10 days — a reminder that a technical setup can be overridden overnight by an earnings surprise.
Status tags:
STRIKE — buy price at/below 200 SMA and RSI ≤ 40 — the strongest combined signal.
Buy zone price at/below 200 SMA, RSI not yet oversold — on trend for a dip-buy, but less confirmation.
Watch price within ~15% above the 200 SMA and RSI under 55 — not cheap yet, worth monitoring.
Momentum price above the 200 SMA, RSI in a healthy mid-range — trending, no extreme in either direction.
Expensive / risk RSI over 65 or price over 25% above the 200 SMA — extended, chasing here carries more downside risk.
Extreme danger RSI over 75 or price over 50% above the 200 SMA — historically stretched; also the tag for a badly broken structural setup.
None of these tags are financial advice — they're a mechanical read of price/momentum only. Always weigh fundamentals, news, and earnings alongside them.
